# Privacy policy

These tools are used only by their owner, with the owner's own Google account.

- Data read from Google (Gmail, Calendar) stays on the owner's own computers.
- Nothing is sold, shared with or sent to third parties.
- No other users exist; the OAuth app is limited to the owner.
- Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the repository owner, via GitHub.
